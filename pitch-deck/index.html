05solution
Core Insight

Memory is the product.

Every existing WhatsApp tool treats messages as a stateless pipe. Nazar treats every contact as a relationship database — and that single architectural choice changes everything downstream.

Without memory

Today's WhatsApp tools

  • "Hi, I'm interested" → AI asks for name, business, intent — every time
  • Customer mentions price objection on Monday → same objection re-surfaces Tuesday with no context
  • Pipeline stage tracked in a spreadsheet, manually, by the owner
  • Team handoff means re-reading a 200-message thread
  • Campaigns blast everyone the same message regardless of history
With memory

Nazar

  • AI retrieves the contact's history before composing the reply
  • Past objections, signals, preferences automatically inform the response
  • Pipeline stage and lead score updated from conversation content
  • Anyone on the team picks up exactly where the AI left off
  • Campaigns can target by deal stage, signal strength, or specific past behaviour
The compounding effect: after 90 days of usage, a customer's memory becomes their most valuable asset. Switching to a stateless tool means losing it. This is the LTV moat — and it grows on its own, with every message.

Meta is removing our competitors

Forced rebuild — or removal from the platform.

Compliant by design

Nazar's architecture

  • Structured sales assistant with explicit knowledge base
  • Defined product/service scope — not open-ended chat
  • Documented handoff triggers to human agents

  • Audit trail and opt-out compliance built in

Already aligned with the new policy. No retrofitting.

What this means: the largest WhatsApp-AI vendors will spend Q1 2026 rebuilding their core product to stay on the platform. Nazar will spend Q1 2026 acquiring their customers.
