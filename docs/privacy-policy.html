Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Nazar is an AI-enabled WhatsApp CRM used to manage customer conversations, leads, follow-ups, and internal sales workflows. This Privacy Policy explains what data Nazar may collect, how it is used, and how deletion requests can be made.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details such as name, phone number, company, and CRM tags.
  • Conversation data sent through WhatsApp, including inbound and outbound messages.
  • CRM data such as pipeline stage, notes, assignments, and follow-up history.
  • Operational usage data such as message status, webhook activity, and system logs.

How We Use Information

  • To send and receive WhatsApp messages for sales, support, and customer communication.
  • To organize contacts, leads, campaigns, and team workflows inside the CRM.
  • To generate AI-assisted replies, summaries, routing suggestions, and follow-up drafts.
  • To improve operational reporting, assignment workflows, and customer response handling.

Third-Party Services

Nazar may use third-party providers such as Meta WhatsApp Business Platform and AI providers including OpenRouter and related model providers. These services may process message content and metadata as required to deliver messaging and AI functionality.

Data Retention

Customer and CRM data may be retained for as long as needed to operate the service, support customer relationships, maintain records, and comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

Nazar does not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers and platform partners necessary to operate messaging, hosting, analytics, and AI features.

Security

Nazar uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored customer and CRM information. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of data processed through Nazar, use the instructions on the data deletion page or contact the operator of the service directly.
