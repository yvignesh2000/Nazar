# SunMitra Solar — Product Knowledge Base

## About SunMitra Solar

SunMitra Solar helps homeowners, apartment associations, schools, clinics, warehouses, and small factories install rooftop solar systems. The company handles consultation, site survey, design, subsidy guidance, installation, net metering coordination, and after-sales support.

Primary service areas:
- Bengaluru
- Mysuru
- Hyderabad

Target customers:
- Homeowners with monthly electricity bills above Rs 4,000
- Businesses with large daytime electricity usage
- Apartment associations exploring common-area solar

## Core Offerings

### Residential Rooftop Solar
- Typical sizes: 3kW, 5kW, 8kW, 10kW
- Best for villas, independent homes, and bungalows
- Goal: reduce monthly bill, improve long-term savings, add backup readiness

### Commercial Rooftop Solar
- Typical sizes: 10kW to 200kW+
- Best for clinics, schools, showrooms, offices, warehouses, and factories
- Goal: reduce operating cost and stabilize long-term power expense

### Free Solar Savings Audit
- Free first consultation
- Preliminary savings estimate based on monthly bill and rooftop type
- Free site survey for qualified leads in serviceable locations

## Pricing Guidance

Exact pricing depends on roof type, approved load, structure, panel choice, inverter brand, and subsidy eligibility.

Reference ranges:

### Residential
- 3kW: Rs 2.1L to Rs 2.6L before subsidy
- 5kW: Rs 3.1L to Rs 3.9L before subsidy
- 8kW: Rs 4.9L to Rs 6.1L before subsidy
- 10kW: Rs 6.0L to Rs 7.5L before subsidy

### Commercial
- 10kW and above: quote-based after technical assessment

Important pricing rules:
- Final commercial quotes are always handled by a human advisor
- If the customer asks for discount, custom quote, AMC bundling, or financing comparison, route to human sales

## Subsidy and Financing

- Residential customers may be eligible for government subsidy depending on current policy and system size
- Commercial customers typically do not get the same subsidy structure as residential users
- EMI / financing options are available through partner lenders for selected profiles
- Financing approval depends on customer profile and documentation

## Process

1. Customer shares location and electricity bill
2. SunMitra shares preliminary savings estimate
3. Qualified lead gets site survey
4. Design + proposal shared
5. Commercial / pricing discussion handled by sales advisor
6. Installation scheduled after approval and payment milestone
7. Net metering coordination and activation

## Timelines

- Initial qualification call: same day or within 24 hours
- Site survey: usually within 2 to 4 days in serviceable areas
- Proposal turnaround: usually 1 to 3 business days after survey
- Residential installation: usually 2 to 7 days after approval
- Net metering approval timeline depends on DISCOM and local process

## Qualification Questions

Ask these early:
- Which city / area is the property in?
- Is it a home or business?
- What is the monthly electricity bill?
- Do they own the roof?
- Is the roof RCC sheet / metal sheet / terrace?
- Are they looking for immediate installation or just exploring?

## FAQ

Q: How much can I save with rooftop solar?
A: Savings depend on system size, bill amount, daytime usage, and roof conditions. A quick savings audit can provide an estimate.

Q: Do you offer free site survey?
A: Yes, for qualified leads in serviceable areas.

Q: Can you help with subsidy?
A: Yes. SunMitra guides eligible residential customers through current subsidy-related steps.

Q: Do you install for businesses?
A: Yes. Commercial and industrial installations are supported, but final sizing and quote are handled by a human advisor.

Q: How long does installation take?
A: Most residential projects are installed within a few days after approval, but approvals and net metering timelines vary.

Q: Can I finance the project on EMI?
A: Financing may be available through lending partners depending on eligibility.
