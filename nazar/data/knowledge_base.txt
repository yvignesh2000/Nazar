## Key Features

### AI Memory
Every customer interaction is remembered. The AI references past conversations naturally, so customers never have to repeat themselves. "Since you mentioned last time that quarterly billing works better..."

### Smart Pipeline
Visual Kanban board with stages: New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost. Deal values, lead scoring, and AI-detected buying signals on every card.

### Broadcasts
Send personalized messages to segments. Unlike competitors, Nazar uses customer memory to personalize each message — not just "Hi {name}" but contextually relevant follow-ups.

### Bot + Human Handoff
AI handles routine conversations. When things get complex — custom pricing, complaints, negotiations — it seamlessly hands off to a human with full context.

### WhatsApp Compliance
Nazar is a structured sales assistant, fully compliant with Meta's Jan 2026 AI chatbot policy. Not a general-purpose chatbot.

## FAQ

Q: Is this compliant with Meta's WhatsApp policies?
A: Yes. Nazar operates as a structured business tool for sales, support, and order management — all explicitly allowed.

Q: Do I need a WhatsApp Business API account?
A: Yes. We help you set it up. If you already have one (through Meta directly or a BSP), that works too.

Q: Can I use my own phone number?
A: Yes. You connect your WhatsApp Business phone number. Customers message your number as usual.

Q: How does pricing compare to WATI/Interakt?
A: We're typically 30-50% cheaper because we charge zero markup on WhatsApp messages. You pay Meta directly for messaging costs.

Q: What languages does the AI support?
A: Hindi, English, Hinglish, and most major Indian languages. The AI auto-detects and responds in the customer's language.

Q: How secure is my data?
A: All customer data is encrypted at rest with per-contact encryption keys. Even if someone accesses the database, individual customer data is unreadable without the master key.
